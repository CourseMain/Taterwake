# Taterland v2.0.4

The computer shop pass, the kit and the cast are now in the browser game. This release includes the local v2.0.2 and v2.0.3 work since the last public v2.0.1 build.

- **Segment 21f:** a quieter, clearer guided year, a 30-second small-storm warning, readable grade explanations, one-row top band, keeper interaction before services, and farmer choices. Hurry is removed. The owner's later changes put buying and selling together at Mara's shop and pause decoration purchases.
- **Segment 21g:** a visible sun and warmer seasonal light, stronger directional shading, drawn Weather/Menu/tool controls, and one solid potato currency glyph.
- **Segment 21h:** the ink, paper and wood kit, Slackey with Atkinson Hyperlegible, keeper voices and matching portraits, and the first three cosmetic clothing rewards. Practice tiles and the sale reveal are presentation components for Segment 22; their gameplay is not activated.
- **Computer shops:** smaller display headings and wider layouts. Whole shop pages stay still under the mouse and animate only on entry and close; individual seed cards retain their hover motion. Mara has Buy/Sell tabs and one current-stock sale control. The barn holds buyer orders and deals.
- **New Farm:** Menu → Save → Plant a new farm now reaches a working cancel/confirm choice. Earned clothing stays across runs.
- **Sea-only entry:** focus and resize events at the title cannot turn the cinematic camera into a farm pan. Fresh Walk fits the overview; Continue restores the farm camera and navigation. The growing hint also clears Weather.
- **Sound and actions:** original tool cues and stronger planting/watering feedback return. Calm ambience is slightly louder. Quieter mutes all sound. Decorations are unavailable at Tools; previously owned items remain in saved farms.

Ledger labels, farm balance and the strategy bot are unchanged.

Validation: **102 / 102 suites pass**, with **0 failures, timeouts or errors**. The required boot passes **44 checks**. Browser checks pass computer Buy/Sell and New Farm controls at three window sizes, nine fresh entries and three saved guided entries, five interaction layouts, and production desktop/phone entry and desktop fullscreen. Further human tester sheets remain pending.
