# Taterland v2.0.1

Fresh **Walk to the farm** now fits the island to the current screen, instead of restoring a camera captured before the first-frame overview fit. Its Home/zoom defaults also use the fitted overview. This fixes the missing or tiny island reported on a fresh launch.

Only the requested title subset of Segment 21f M accompanies part A: Mara, Bram and the farmer breathe slowly and blink every 3–5 seconds; a few blossom petals drift across Spring titles. This motion ends at entry. No new text or sound. Other parts of Segment 21f are not implemented.

Play: https://coursemain.github.io/Taterwake/

Download **Taterland-Web.zip**, extract the entire archive, and use the Play Web launcher. Classic v1.0.3.1 remains at https://coursemain.github.io/Taterwake/classic/ and in the v2.0.0 release assets.
