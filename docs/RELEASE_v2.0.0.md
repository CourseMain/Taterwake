# Taterland v2.0.0

The big-number market game becomes a ten-year farming survival game. Every Winter records real earnings and bills; climate pressure grows through the run. The round potato farmer, villagers, handmade island and cream paper identity remain.

Play: https://coursemain.github.io/Taterwake/

Classic v1.0.3.1: https://coursemain.github.io/Taterwake/classic/

Downloads: **Taterland-Web.zip** (v2.0.0) and **Taterland-Classic-Web.zip** (v1.0.3.1). Extract the full ZIP and use its Play Web launcher.
