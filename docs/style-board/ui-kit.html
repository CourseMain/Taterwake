Taterland UI kit

Reference mockups for Segment 21h, tokens brightened for the vibrancy pass (21i F). Codex matches these side by side, screen for screen. Every colour and face on this page is the game's own; nothing here is a suggestion.

The three rules under every mockup: ink felt is the ground, paper carries facts, wood frames. One drawing above every name. No grey, no white, no cream on cream, no text under 14 px on a phone.

Surfaces and connection colours

Everything about one thing shares one colour, everywhere it appears. Golden is gold on its seed card, its stamp, its sale line and its catalogue entry. Each keeper owns a colour used on their page header, portrait frame and Winter lines.

Two typefaces, pick the display face

One display face for names, numbers and signs; one plain body face. The five bundled fonts go. Three display candidates below; the body face is the same in all three. Pick on a phone, then it is fixed for the whole game.

Nell's books · 104,000A. Slackey. Chunky, hand-cut, sign-painter. Closest to the wooden gate sign.
Nell's books · 104,000B. Fraunces 900 wonky. Soft serif with a wobble. Warmer, more bookish.
Nell's books · 104,000C. Gaegu bold. Handwritten with a pen. Loosest, friendliest, least sign-like.

Body in all three: Atkinson Hyperlegible, 16 px, with 14 px captions and 22 px headings. Nothing smaller on a phone.

The market board

All five potatoes at once, one row each, on Mara's Sell page and on the pier board. A row is a drawing, the name, how many tonnes you hold, a jagged 90-second line, and the current price with the last tick's arrow. Tap a row to pick it; one sell bar at the bottom serves the picked row. No button inside a row, no page per potato. A boom stamps its multiplier beside the name.

What gets a screen sent back

Grey or white anywhere. Ink, paper, wood only.
A word pill where a picture fits. Hats, shirts, tools, crops, keepers are drawn.
Text under 14 px at 390 wide, or more than three sizes on a screen.
A scrollbar on a card. Cards fit; pages scroll.
A rarity word or star. The edge colour is the rarity.
A third typeface.
A keeper line out of voice or longer than eight words.
